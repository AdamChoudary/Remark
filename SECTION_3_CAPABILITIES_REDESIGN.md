## 2. Key Enhancements Over The Previous Design

| Aspect | Old Design | New Ground-Up Redesign |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Viewport Container** | Boxed rounded white card (`bg-white/40 border rounded-2xl`) | **Unboxed Full-Bleed Spatial Canvas** integrated directly into the paper register. |
| **Typography Scale** | Standard small list headings | **Monumental Cranio Display Names** (`clamp(2rem, 3.5vw, 3.25rem)`). |
| **Micro-Interactions** | Simple left indicator line | **Kinetic Lerp Thermal Reticle** + self-drawing SVG contour accents. |
| **Visualizer Staging** | Boxed inside a sticky square box | **Floating Ambient Depth Canvas** that blends organically with the negative space. |
