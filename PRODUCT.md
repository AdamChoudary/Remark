# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B decision-makers (founders, CEOs, marketing leads) in Islamabad and across Pakistan who need web development, AI voice agents, chatbots, CRM/ERP, and digital marketing services. They value professionalism, modern technology, and evidence of delivery.

## Product Purpose

Remark Studio is an Islamabad-based digital solutions agency. The site exists to build credibility, demonstrate capability, and convert visitors into leads. Success = inbound inquiries that lead to signed projects.

## Brand Personality

Bold, editorial, precise, confident. Not friendly start-up; not corporate cold. A design studio that happens to code. Cinematic register, engineered precision — red is not a highlight color here, it is the studio's material: the thing the brand is built from, the way molten metal or ink is a material for a foundry. Authoritative typography, one dominant idea per screen, nothing decorative standing in for content that doesn't exist yet.

## Anti-references

- Purple/blue SaaS gradient slop with generic hero + 3 cards
- Inter or Roboto as default fonts
- Centered everything with no spatial risk
- Glassmorphism, emoji icons, bouncing scroll cues
- Warm cream/beige craft palette used as a blanket "whole site goes cream" move, or as a shy pastel instead of a committed material — light sections are legitimate (see DESIGN.md), but only when a real, dominant image or a saturated red field carries the section. A tasteful cream card with a small boxed illustration is exactly the slop this rule bans.
- Same layout repeated across sections
- Decorative chrome standing in for missing content: empty placeholder frames dressed as art, coordinate/caption text with no real meaning, corner registration ticks around nothing. If the enrichment can be deleted and the section still works, it earned its place; if deleting it leaves an obvious hole, it was never real content.
- The generic floating-glass-pill nav (rounded island, backdrop blur, corner flourishes) — the most recognizable 2024-25 AI nav shape.
- A hero assembled from many small timid elements (rail + eyebrow + two-tone headline + arrow glyph + subtitle + button, each competing quietly) instead of one decisive, dominant gesture.

## Design Principles

- **One mark per moment.** Exactly one red-marked word per section, revealed on scroll.
- **Layouts never repeat.** Every section in the sequence uses a distinct layout family.
- **Matter over decoration.** Every visual element serves credibility or clarity — never just "looks good." If it can be deleted and the section still works, it wasn't load-bearing.
- **One dominant gesture per section.** A section has ONE thing the eye lands on — a real image, a giant number, a real quote — not an assembly of several small decorated parts.
- **Texture builds depth, never fills absence.** SVG patterns, grain, and architectural grids add richness *on top of* real content; they are never used to occupy space where real content (an image, a headline) is still pending.

Theme register (dark vs. light, imagery approach, color mechanics) is visual system, not strategy — see `DESIGN.md`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast minimum
- Reduced motion honored via `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Focus-visible indicators throughout
- Skip-to-content link
- Semantic HTML structure
